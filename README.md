Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-swift-raven-16-alien-field-notes-the-two-day-earth-7647b000) competition **"Alien Field Notes: The Two Day Earth Review"**.

One line: an alien tourist's review of Earth, verdict and rating included, in the visitor's outside voice.

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Two days on Earth: the locals queue politely for coffee, scream at their own reflections in video calls, and worship a glowing rectangle — 3/5 stars, would orbit again.

The text is 168 characters (UTF-8, within the 1–200 limit), one line, no trailing newline.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
```
